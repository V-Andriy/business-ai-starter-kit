# Business Writing

## First Output

Deliver a usable draft early, using the audience, purpose, and source material provided. A one-off draft can stay in the conversation or one requested file.

## Clarify Only What Matters

Identify the recipient, desired action, tone, channel, and length. Use a sensible default when these are clear from context. Ask when uncertainty changes a promise, price, claim, or sensitive disclosure.

## Draft

Lead with the reader's need and a concrete point. Use plain language, specific evidence, and one clear next action. Preserve the user's voice. Avoid inflated claims, false urgency, invented testimonials, or unsupported guarantees.

## Sales And Proposals

Separate confirmed scope and terms from placeholders. Mark missing prices, dates, responsibilities, and approval conditions. Do not invent customer facts or imply agreement. Keep internal negotiation notes outside the external draft.

## Quality Check

Check names, numbers, dates, links, factual claims, and the requested action. Remove sensitive material not approved for this audience. For revisions, explain only material changes or unresolved questions; do not supply multiple variants unless they help a real choice.

## Boundary

Drafting does not authorize sending, publishing, uploading, or contacting a recipient. Use root approval rules before any external action. Save a reusable pattern only when the user repeats this workflow.
